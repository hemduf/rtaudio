RtAudio WebAudio playsaw

Browser equivalent of tests/playsaw.cpp. The page owns the AudioContext and explicitly initializes the RtAudio WebAudio runtime with it before any stream is opened. The left channel plays the selected frequency and the right channel plays 1.5× that value. The sliders demonstrate main-thread → AudioWorklet communication; the stereo meter demonstrates the opposite path. The RtAudio stream is exposed as a real WebAudio node and connected through a browser GainNode.
